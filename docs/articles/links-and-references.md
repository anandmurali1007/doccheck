## Checklist

1. Relative links between synced articles resolve to the synced articles.
2. Repository-relative links are rewritten or left intact consistently.
3. External links open unchanged.
4. Anchor links scroll within the article instead of navigating away.
5. Reference-style definitions are resolved and not printed as literal text.
